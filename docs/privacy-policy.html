LifeHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

LifeHub ("the app") is an all-in-one personal organizer for Android. This policy explains, in plain terms, how the app handles your information.

The short version

LifeHub collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. The app works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.

What the app stores, and where

All of the following is stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Finance data — transactions, budgets, savings goals
  • Tasks, notes, and habits — including their reminder schedules
  • Vault entries — passwords and notes encrypted with AES-256-GCM; the encryption key is held in Android's secure keystore, not on disk in plain text
  • Security settings — your unlock PIN, stored only as an Argon2id hash, and your biometric-unlock preference
  • Backups — optional, encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a passphrase you choose; the file is created only when you tap "Export backup" and goes only where you send it

Permissions, and what they're for

PermissionPurpose
USE_BIOMETRICOptional fingerprint unlock. Fingerprint data is managed entirely by Android; the app never sees or stores it.
POST_NOTIFICATIONSDeliver the reminders you schedule for tasks and habits.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDRe-register your reminders after the device restarts.

The app does not use the internet permission — it cannot send your data anywhere, by design.

Photos and files are accessed only when you explicitly pick them (profile photo, backup restore, PDF report sharing) through Android's own picker or share sheet. Camera access is used only when you choose "Take photo" for your profile picture.

Data sharing

None. There is nothing to share — no data ever leaves your device except files you export yourself (backups, PDF reports) through the share targets you choose.

Your control over your data

  • Export — Settings → Backup → export creates an encrypted backup file you keep
  • Delete — Settings → Clear all data erases everything on-device; uninstalling the app also removes all data
  • Backups you exported are under your control — keep or delete the files as you like

Security

  • Vault and backup data are encrypted with AES-256-GCM
  • Backup keys are derived from your passphrase with Argon2id
  • PINs are hashed with Argon2id and never stored in plain text
  • Vault keys live in Android's hardware-backed secure storage

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same location and the "Last updated" date will change.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach the developer through the contact email listed on the app's Play Store page.
