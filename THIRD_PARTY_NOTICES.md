## Flutter and package dependencies

Flutter collects the LICENSE files from Flutter and pub dependencies into the
application's generated `NOTICES` asset. LifeHub exposes those notices through
the Settings → Open source licenses screen. The bundled font attributions above
are registered separately by the application because the font files are local
assets rather than pub packages.
